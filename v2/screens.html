Hotel app and ops dashboard

Responsive web (tablet and desktop). Same tokens and components, so a Flutter Web or React build both work.

SAGA for Hotels
  1. Today: the car revealed on a landscape stage, KPIs that count up, today's guest rides, live map
  2. Book a ride: guest, room, flight (scan the ticket or pick it), payment mode, quote with commission, WhatsApp preview
  3. Live tracking: the guest's car on its route, progress steps, chauffeur, the message the guest received
  4. Bookings: filters, detail drawer with map, resend / cancel
  5. Commission and statements: commission by month, payment mix, netting with GST/TDS, past statements
  6. Settings: property, staff access, partner terms, guest perks
Ops dashboard
  1. Command: lit KPIs, fleet map with car sprites and IDs, alerts with one-click actions, flight timeline, live waitlist, charging bays
  2. Bookings: live, scheduled, waitlist
  3. Flight watch: today's flight timeline, every linked flight and how its pickup moved
  4. Fleet and chauffeurs: status, charge, location, next job
  5. Hotel partners: rides, gross, rate, commission
  6. Offers: running offers, budget caps, new-offer builder
  7. Notifications: event catalogue with channels and templates in 3 languages
