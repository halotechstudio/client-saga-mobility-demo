SAGA MOBILITY · v2.3

Screen schema and flows

Every screen in the four apps, how they connect, and what each one needs from the backend. Wireframes are the live screens rendered in greyscale, so they never drift from the prototype. The prototype is at index.html.

How to read this

Boxes in each diagram are screens, arrows are taps that lead to another screen. Click a box to jump to its wireframe and notes. Tab-bar destinations are reachable from any screen in the same app.

Colour has a meaning

Only three colours carry the product, used the same way in every app.

White is room. Surfaces and reading. Calm.
Black is trust and place. Identity, the car, the fare, what is selected.
Neon blue is move. The next action, and anything live: route, tracking, ride code.

rider/arrived

Airport

Airport rides

Two simple ways in. A lit glass card to scan or upload the ticket; or Vizag's own departures (or arrivals) board for the chosen day and city, with split-flap times that settle into place. The flight already caught by the Gmail filter sits on top.

Why

No airline lets apps look a PNR up, so SAGA asks for what it can check instantly: the ticket itself, or the flight. Vizag has one airport and a short daily list, so a real board is the fastest picker.

Build and API

Scan: mobile_scanner (boarding-pass barcode, IATA BCBP) + google_mlkit_text_recognition, on device. Board: GET /flights/board?airport=VTZ&date=&dir= from the flight-schedule API; flap digits are an AnimatedSwitcher per character.

Airport

Scan ticket

A camera viewfinder over the ticket. A soft scan light sweeps down; each field it reads (PNR, flight, date, route, time, passengers) locks with a cyan frame and ticks into a list. Then the found flight slides up as a pass, ready to plan the pickup.

Why

Works for every airline and travel site, offline, in about a second, with nothing to type. Seeing each field lock builds trust that the app read the right flight.

Build and API

mobile_scanner for PDF417 / Aztec boarding-pass barcodes (IATA BCBP); google_mlkit_text_recognition for e-tickets and screenshots (text blocks give the lock rectangles); file_picker for PDFs. All on device; only the flight fields go to the server.
