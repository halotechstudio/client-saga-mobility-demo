SAGA MOBILITY · v2.3

Screen schema and flows

Every screen in the four apps, how they connect, and what each one needs from the backend. Wireframes are the live screens rendered in greyscale, so they never drift from the prototype. The prototype is at index.html.

How to read this

Boxes in each diagram are screens, arrows are taps that lead to another screen. Click a box to jump to its wireframe and notes. Tab-bar destinations are reachable from any screen in the same app.

Colour has a meaning

Only three colours carry the product, used the same way in every app.

White is room. Surfaces and reading. Calm.
Black is trust and place. Identity, the car, the fare, what is selected.
Neon blue is move. The next action, and anything live: route, tracking, ride code.

Airport

Airport rides

Two simple ways in. Scan or upload the ticket and the app reads the flight; or pick the date and city and tap your flight from Vizag's short daily list. No PNR needed.

Why

No airline lets apps look a PNR up, so SAGA asks for what it can check instantly: the ticket itself, or the flight. Both take seconds and work for every airline.

Build and API

Scan: mobile_scanner (boarding-pass barcode, IATA BCBP) + google_mlkit_text_recognition, on device. Pick: GET /flights/board?airport=VTZ&amp;date= from the flight-schedule API.

rider/airport

Airport

Scan ticket

Camera view (or a picked screenshot or PDF). On-device text recognition reads the PNR, flight, date and names; a boarding-pass barcode is read exactly. The found flight appears for one-tap confirmation.

Why

Works for every airline and travel site, offline, in about a second, with nothing to type.

Build and API

mobile_scanner for PDF417 / Aztec boarding-pass barcodes (IATA BCBP); google_mlkit_text_recognition for e-tickets and screenshots; file_picker for PDFs. All on device; only the flight fields go to the server.

Airport

Pickup plan

The flight as a boarding pass, then a plain timeline. Departures: pickup, airport arrival, check-in close, take-off, with the pickup adjustable ±5 min. Arrivals: landing, chauffeur at the kerb, free wait. Flight tracking is on by default.

Why

Shows the working, so riders trust an automatic time. Delays move the pickup without the rider doing anything.

Build and API

Pickup = departure − buffer (domestic 90 min, intl 180 min) − drive time (Routes API, traffic for that hour). Stored as a rule, recomputed on every flight-status update.
